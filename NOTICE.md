is a Government work under **section 17(d) of the Copyright Act, 1957**, and its reproduction is
permitted by **section 52(1)(q)**. The licences above cover the compilers' selection, extraction
and structuring of that text, not the law. NyayaVeda AI claims no right in the law and grants none.

**Personal data.** Every Aadhaar number, PAN, phone number and personal name injected into the
personal-data questions is synthetic. The numbers were generated in the real formats, so any one of
